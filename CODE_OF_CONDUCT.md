# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Keyturn to be a welcoming project for everyone — MSP owners, solo
admins, first-time contributors, and seasoned engineers alike — regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful of differing opinions and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use Keyturn

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — including customer or tenant data —
  without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **[support@solve4x.ai](mailto:support@solve4x.ai)**.
All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.
